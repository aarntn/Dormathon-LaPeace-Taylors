**Goal:** Complete build guide Prompt 3, then stop at the phone `/test-eyes` checkpoint.

**Architecture:** A plain TypeScript signal engine owns rolling windows, calibration and movement gating. A local MediaPipe worker and browser session own camera, location, audio and wake lock; a React provider keeps the session alive across Drive transitions. No backend or frame upload.

**Tech Stack:** Existing React/Vite/TypeScript stack, prescribed `@mediapipe/tasks-vision`, browser APIs, Node tests and existing Chromium review harness.

## Global constraints

- Front camera 640 × 480; local FaceLandmarker VIDEO with blendshapes, GPU first, about 15 FPS.
- Calibration: five seconds looking ahead, two completed blinks, personal midpoint threshold.
- Tired: PERCLOS >15% in 60 seconds, mean blink >400 ms in 60 seconds, or three yawns in five minutes. Yawn: jawOpen >0.6 for >1.5 seconds.
- Microsleep: eyes continuously closed at least 1.5 seconds; alarm only after speed >2.8 m/s for five seconds. Lost: no face for three seconds.
- All thresholds in one configurable object. Numeric features every 30 seconds. No backend.
- Alarm persists until acknowledgement. Hidden demo movement switch and debug; test page explicitly shows debug.
- Existing Figma components/tokens and screen transitions. Stop after Prompt 3; no commits requested.

## Review focus

- Missing face, stalled camera or long frame gap breaks continuous-closure evidence.
- Null, invalid, stale or denied GPS cannot authorise a moving alarm.
- Late camera/model/wake-lock results after stop must release resources.
- Acknowledgement during closed eyes must require reopening before rearming.
- Rejected audio/camera/model permissions must be visible, with retry and cleanup.

## Execution rulings

- Unknown frames are excluded from the PERCLOS denominator; the ratio is time-weighted. A configurable five-second valid-evidence minimum prevents one startup blink from immediately producing a tired warning.
- Missing or stale GPS closes the moving gate. A latched alarm continues until acknowledgement, even if speed subsequently drops.
- Calibration collects valid open-eye evidence for five seconds and two completed blinks. Missing face resets the current uninterrupted calibration step.
- Detection works in a local worker to avoid synchronous inference blocking the UI. GPU is attempted first; unsupported devices may use CPU with that status shown.
- Test page stays on screen while displaying state. Drive mode routes to warning screens. Production debug and pretend movement stay in the hidden demo panel.
- Local model/WASM enable ongoing offline sessions; a production service worker caches the app and model for subsequent offline launches. First loading still requires a connection.
- Physical phone camera, night/car lighting and OS background suspension require user device checks; synthetic browser verification cannot establish these.


## Review fixes

- Frozen frames: both callback paths check media time before capture. Regression fails with old rAF behaviour.
- GPS: freshness is measured from original position timestamp; movement needs fixes spanning five seconds. Regression fails when callback arrival renews an old fix.
- Wake lock: pending acquisition is guarded; late sentinels release after stop. Regression fails without the pending guard.
- Detection failure: latched alarm and wake lock persist until acknowledgement; restart is offered on Drive/lost screens. Regression fails when failure disposes the alarm.
- Late callbacks: generation guards cover camera, bitmap, worker error, GPS, voice loading, audio unlock and wake-lock results.
